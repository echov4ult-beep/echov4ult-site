A department, not a prompt pack

In simple terms: you buy text files. Each one teaches your AI assistant to play a specific marketing role — a department head plus five specialists. You talk to them like staff; they plan and draft; you approve and publish.

The provenance, plainly: Launch Crew was distilled from the agent team that ran our own product launch. These are the cleaned-up, generalized versions of the roles we actually used — our private business details removed and replaced with an interview that learns your business instead. We're telling you where it came from, not promising what it will earn you.

Two editions ship in every tier: the Claude Code edition (agents that load into your project) and a Universal edition (the same 6 agents as plain system prompts for claude.ai Projects or any other AI assistant).

What Launch Crew does NOT do

Read this before buying — it's the honest edge of the product, and it's deliberate:

  • No API integrations. Nothing connects to Meta, Google, TikTok, X, your email service, or any ad platform. There are no keys to configure because there are no integrations.
  • No auto-posting, no auto-sending. Ever. The agents have no publishing access of any kind.
  • Drafts and plans only — you publish. Every post, email, and article ends up queued as a draft flagged for your approval. Your hand is on the button, always.
  • No outcome promises. These agents plan and draft; marketing results depend on your product, your market, and your execution. Full disclaimers ship in the pack — you own compliance (ad-platform policies, email laws, FTC disclosure) for anything you choose to publish or send.

If you've seen "AI marketing automation" tools that post on your behalf, this is built on the opposite bet: an AI should never speak as your business without you reading it first. You will need your own Claude account (a paid Claude subscription, or a Claude Console account with credits) to run it — that is a separate cost, not included here.

Who this is NOT for

If you want software that posts and emails automatically while you sleep, this is the wrong product on purpose — see the list above. And if you have no product, service, or audience goal yet, the CMO will interview you and find nothing to plan around; come back when there's something to market.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Nothing in Launch Crew involves writing code. QUICK_START assumes you have never opened a terminal: it defines its three jargon terms up front, shows the exact command for every step and what success looks like, and includes a troubleshooting box per step. If you can follow a recipe, you can install this.

Will this grow my revenue?

We won't promise that, and you should distrust anyone selling AI marketing who does. Launch Crew gives you what a marketing department gives you — a plan built around your actual business, a maintained pipeline, drafts ready for your approval, and a weekly review so it doesn't rot. Results depend on your product, your market, and your execution; the disclaimers in the pack say the same thing in more words. What we can say: it converts "I should be doing marketing" into specific work, queued and waiting for you.

What is a "marketing agent," concretely?

A markdown text file containing a detailed role definition. Claude Code reads it and takes on that role — its scope, its methods, its boundaries — for the conversation. You can open every file in any text editor and read exactly what your department believes.

Do I need Claude Code?

It's the best experience — agents that persist in your project, files they maintain for you. But every tier includes the Universal edition: the same agents as plain system prompts you can paste into claude.ai Projects or any other capable AI assistant. Either way you need your own paid Claude account (or another AI service for the Universal edition) — not included.

Will it post to my accounts or send my emails?

No, and it can't. There are no integrations, no keys, no publishing access. Everything ends as a draft flagged for your approval. This is a feature, not a roadmap gap.

How is the free Demo limited?

It's the real CMO agent, complete — interview, plan, board, weekly review, and solo-mode drafting. What it lacks is the department: the five specialists it would otherwise delegate to, and the Pro playbooks. If the CMO alone doesn't change how you work in a week, don't buy the upgrade.

Aren't there free marketing agents on GitHub?

Yes, good ones — big free repositories include marketing agent files, and if a single prompt file is all you need, use them. Free repos give you agent files. Launch Crew is a wired department: a CMO that interviews you and writes your BUSINESS.md, delegation and weekly reviews across six coordinated roles, four playbooks the agents actually follow, two editions, and a beginner ramp that takes a non-technical owner from nothing installed to a working conversation without help. That's what the price buys.

Is my business information sent anywhere?

The pack itself is inert text files — it transmits nothing. Your interview answers live in a marketing/BUSINESS.md file in your own project folder. Conversations with the agents are handled by your AI provider under its terms, same as any other use of your Claude account.

What's the license?

Demo: personal evaluation. Core ($39): single seat, one person. Pro ($79): up to five authorized members of one company or organized team, plus v1.x updates. Full license texts ship in the pack.
