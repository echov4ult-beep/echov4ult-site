In the box

The 8 skills

/handover

The quality floor

A retiring senior operator's complete way-of-thinking: interpret what a request actually asks, verify instead of pattern-match, grade your own confidence out loud, self-review before answering — plus a named bestiary of AI failure modes ("victory at compile," "silent recovery," "the agreement reflex").

/lean-mode

The spend ceiling

Session-wide token discipline: batch your asks, single-pass execution, max 2 fix-verify loops per task, no exploration tours, one-command validation instead of full app launches. Cuts waste — never validation.

/lean-start

Both, one command

Loads the quality floor and the spend ceiling together, then asks for your batch. This is the whole workflow.

/grill-me

The front door

A relentless interview until you and the AI share the same mental model, before any code or spec exists.

/mull

Bounded brainstorming

A persistent ledger: diverge → cull → deepen, max 3 rounds, stops when dry, ends with the decisions only you can make.

/coach

Prompt-craft coaching

Before/after rewrites of your own real prompts, one principle at a time, logged to a Prompt Dojo ledger so lessons never repeat.

/session-close

The end-of-arc ritual

Land the work, update memory and status, print a handoff paragraph — then start fresh instead of paying mega-session prices.

/usage-audit

The scoreboard

A bundled Python script makes one pass over your full local transcript history: tool/skill/model usage, session-length distribution, friction points — then concrete fixes. Run it monthly.

How they interlock

Eight skills, one loop

lean-start boots the quality floor and the spend ceiling together. New idea? grill-me aligns it; mull develops it without babysitting. coach watches your prompts and makes you better at all of the above. session-close ends the arc; usage-audit tells you monthly whether any of this is working — in numbers, from your own history. That's the product.

Setup

Two placeholders, two minutes

Your notes folder and your project's one-command validation. Install via plugin (one command) or copy a folder. QUICK_START.md gets you to your first lean session in five minutes and includes a two-week habit ladder.

The honest part

  • These are prompts and process, not magic. They're designed to cut token waste and rework; results depend on your projects and habits.
  • We use them daily in our own studio's workflow — that's the claim, and the whole claim. AI assistants make mistakes; you remain responsible for reviewing what they produce.
  • usage-audit and session-close are Claude Code-specific by nature; four skills ship as universal ports for other tools, but two don't, and we say so rather than pretend otherwise.

Who this is NOT for

If you use an AI assistant occasionally and your bills don't sting, you don't need this — and if you're looking for coding-methodology content (TDD workflows, framework recipes), the big free repositories already do that well.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Why pay $19 when huge free skill repos exist?

Different job. The free catalogs (some with enormous stars, deservedly) are dev-methodology libraries — hundreds of skills to browse and sift. This is eight skills designed together as one operating discipline: a floor, a ceiling, a front door, an ending, and a monthly scoreboard. You're not buying skill count; you're buying the interlock — plus sanitized docs, an install path, and a system already run daily in a working studio.

Does this work outside Claude Code?

Partly, and we'll be precise: Agent Skills is an open standard, and four of the skills ship as universal plain-text ports (handover, grill-me, mull, and the coach curriculum) you can paste into any capable AI chat. usage-audit and session-close are Claude Code-specific by nature, and we say so rather than pretend otherwise.

Will this actually lower my token bill?

It's designed to — capped retry loops, batched asks, no exploration tours, one-command validation instead of full app launches. We won't quote a savings number we haven't measured for you. That's what usage-audit is for: run it before and after your first month and get your own answer.

Is the free demo crippled?

No. It's the complete, unabridged handover skill — the pack's quality floor — plus its universal plain-text port. If it doesn't visibly change your sessions, don't buy the kit.

What do I need to set it up?

Claude Code, plus two find-replace placeholders: a notes folder (any folder works; an Obsidian vault is ideal) and your project's one-command validation script. Both install paths are in INSTALL.md; a paste-ready CLAUDE.md snippet is included.

What's the license?

One purchaser, unlimited personal and commercial projects, no redistribution. Full text ships in the pack.
