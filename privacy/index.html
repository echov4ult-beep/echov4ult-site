Privacy

Privacy, in plain English

Effective 2026-08-01 · EchoVault, LLC

What this site collects: almost nothing

echov4ult.com is a static website. We set no cookies and use no ad tech or cross-site tracking. We don't collect, store, or sell personal information through this site. The one thing we do count is page views, using GoatCounter — a privacy-first analytics tool that sets no cookies, doesn't follow you across sites, and doesn't build a profile of you. It tells us which pages get visited, and that's it (see GoatCounter's privacy policy).

The fine print we can't skip

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log standard request data (like IP addresses) to operate the service — see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google's servers — see Google's privacy policy.
  • The game: V4ULT RUN saves your best score in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data deletes it.

YouTube API Services

We operate a separate application ("Velvet Owl Publisher") that uses the YouTube API Services to manage our own YouTube channel, The Velvet Owl. This application:

  • Accesses: our own channel's public statistics (subscriber count, view counts, video metadata) and upload functionality. It does not access any other user's YouTube account or data.
  • Uses data for: scheduling video uploads, tracking channel performance on an internal dashboard, and pulling analytics for our own content strategy. No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
  • Stores: aggregated channel statistics (views, likes, subscriber counts) locally for up to 36 months to track channel growth. Video metadata (titles, publish dates) is refreshed regularly. No personally identifiable viewer data is collected or stored.
  • Google's privacy policy applies to data obtained through YouTube API Services — see Google's Privacy Policy.

You can revoke our application's access to YouTube data at any time via Google's security settings.

When you buy, subscribe, or email us

  • Purchases happen on Gumroad, which handles payment under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details. Gumroad shares your email and order details with us so we can deliver support and product updates.
  • Newsletter signups are handled by Beehiiv under its privacy policy; every issue includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Email: if you write to us, we keep the correspondence so we can help you. We don't add you to any list you didn't join.

Your choices

Want your data deleted from anything we control (support emails, order records we hold)? Email echov4ult@gmail.com and we'll handle it. Depending on where you live, you may also have legal rights to access, correct, export, or object to the use of your personal data (under laws like the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA) — email us and we'll honor any that apply. For data held by Gumroad, Beehiiv, GitHub, or Google, their policies above are the right door.

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Questions welcome — contact us.
