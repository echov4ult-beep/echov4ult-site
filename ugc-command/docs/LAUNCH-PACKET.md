# Christopher's controlled-launch packet

## Personal setup checklist

- [ ] Approve the three package prices and every separately quoted usage add-on.
- [ ] Choose and configure the payment account; until then, record only payments you verified yourself.
- [ ] Approve a transactional-email provider and templates, or accept that every reply and intake invitation is manual.
- [ ] Have the agreement, privacy policy, usage-rights language, cancellation terms, and AI-production terms reviewed by a qualified professional.
- [ ] Choose five truthful portfolio products; record the footage, founder photo, short biography, and permission status for each.
- [ ] Approve the first prospect list and every first-contact pitch individually.
- [ ] Prepare a short sales-call outline: client goal, deliverables, usage, deadline, claims, assets, revisions, price, and next action.
- [ ] Confirm one restorable database backup before accepting the first customer.

## Daily founder check — 15 to 30 minutes

1. Open Founder Attention and handle urgent approvals.
2. Review prospect replies and sales conversations.
3. Approve, reject, or request changes on pitch drafts.
4. Resolve client, claim, contract, payment, or delivery blockers.
5. Check footage requests and money requiring attention.

## Weekly founder check

1. Record approved footage and attend scheduled sales calls.
2. Review client drafts and outstanding revisions.
3. Read the Friday growth review without letting it publish or send anything.
4. Approve one main objective for the next week.
5. Reconcile collected revenue against unpaid invoices.

## First-customer playbook

- **First inquiry:** verify it in the intake gateway, copy only the needed facts into UGC Command, qualify it, then draft a response. Do not send until the approval is marked approved.
- **Prospect replies:** record the reply, move the lead to `REPLIED`, and decide the next human-approved response.
- **Call requested:** use the sales-call outline, record decisions afterward, and do not promise rights or claims that are absent from the agreement.
- **Proposal accepted:** mark the lead won, create the client project, record the accepted agreement, create the deposit invoice, and confirm payment only after checking the payment account.
- **Product arrives:** complete asset and shipping fields, then unpause production only for the work you are ready to start.
- **Revision requested:** record the request against the delivered version. If it exceeds the signed allowance, the scope-warning item must be resolved before more work.
- **Client approves:** attach the final unwatermarked version, run version-specific QA, request final-delivery approval, and confirm the remaining payment.
- **Paid usage requested:** quote and record the paid-ad add-on plus duration. Never treat paid usage, perpetual rights, raw footage, or exclusivity as included.
- **Retainer declined:** record the outcome, keep the relationship in a permission-safe follow-up state, and do not automate repeated contact.
