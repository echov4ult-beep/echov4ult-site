# EchoVault UGC growth plan — plain English

Verified 2026-09-05. The $6,000–$7,000 figure is a target, not a forecast or guarantee.

## The simple business model

EchoVault sells short product videos to brands. The first $149 project makes it easy for a brand to try the service. A successful trial should lead to a larger $399 project and then a recurring monthly package. Extra usage rights are charged separately. Affiliate revenue is optional upside, not money the business depends on.

| Source | Simple math | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Four recurring clients | 4 × about $1,250 | $5,000 |
| Two one-off projects | 2 × about $499 | $998 |
| Paid usage and add-ons | Separate rights/services | $700 |
| Affiliate commission | Secondary experiments | $300 |
| Total | | **$6,998 gross** |

“Gross” means money collected before software, products, contractors, taxes, and Christopher’s labor. The dashboard therefore tracks direct costs and production time too. Views, followers, predicted virality, unsigned proposals, and unpaid invoices are not revenue.

## The easiest path to the first customer

1. Finish five honest portfolio samples covering gaming, home office, creator equipment, technology unboxing, and an AI/software demo. Mark unpaid examples as self-initiated portfolio work.
2. Research real brands with demonstrable products, active marketing, a verified contact route, and a specific creative opportunity.
3. Prepare a short personalized pitch and recommend the smallest useful package. Christopher approves and sends every message manually.
4. Sell the $149 test. Collect the agreed payment, confirm product claims and usage rights, then produce and deliver through the gated workflow.
5. Ask for performance data, a testimonial, portfolio permission, and a second project. Prepare two new concepts and a retainer recommendation.
6. Repeat what converts. Do not automate outreach until manual pitches have produced customers.

## Rules that protect the business

- Internal research and drafting may continue while one item waits for approval.
- No invented contacts, testimonials, experience, performance, or revenue.
- No outreach, publishing, contract, spend, price change, or final delivery without the proper owner gate.
- No unpaid unwatermarked final.
- No perpetual rights or exclusivity hidden inside a base price.
- Autonomy begins at prepare/internal-execution levels only. Higher levels require 20 successful supervised runs, zero material incidents and duplicate actions, acceptable error rate, complete audit, a tested emergency stop, and a specific owner-approved promotion record.
