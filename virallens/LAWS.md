# ViralLens house laws

Laws are non-negotiable rules for every video ViralLens scores. A pre-publish
cut that breaks a law is flagged before anything else is checked, and every
connected TikTok video is graded against them on the calibration page.

The machine-readable copy lives in `laws.json` and is what the page loads.
Keep the two in sync.

## Law 1 — Open with the Seven / Zero question

**Rule:** every video opens with the question
**"What if Seven and Zero swapped teams?"**

**Why:** the old videos that pulled the highest view counts all started with
this exact question. It is the proven hook, so it is the default opener until
the numbers say otherwise.

**Check:** the spoken opener, the on-screen text, and the caption all lead with
the question before anything else. Accepted forms:

- What if Seven and Zero swapped teams?
- What if Seven and Zero swap teams?
- What if 7 and 0 switched teams?

Leading emoji, hashtags, and punctuation are ignored when the caption is
checked. Anything else in front of the question is a miss.
