About

One builder, a crew of agents, receipts for everything

echov4ult is the workshop of Chris Retherford, operating as EchoVault, LLC. By day I work in data; nights and weekends I build games and the AI systems that help build them — and I package the parts that survive real use into kits other builders can install.

What we make

The flagship is Full Party, an AI game studio kit for Claude Code: ten specialist agents, a governance system, thirty workflows, and a tested installer. It exists because I kept rebuilding the same agent roster for every project and finally did it properly once. More kits are on the way — the roadmap follows what actually survives use in my own projects.

How we work

  • Nothing ships checked once. Every product passes an adversarial second review before it goes on sale — the installer's acceptance suite (22/22 scenarios) runs fresh before any release claim.
  • Receipts over hype. Numbers in our marketing come from real dashboards and logs. When we have zero customers, the page says zero customers. Flat days get posted too.
  • Built in public. The blog and the AI Onramp newsletter document the whole experiment, including what breaks.

The honest part

This is a small operation: one human making the decisions, with AI agents doing a lot of the drafting, checking, and plumbing under written rules and human approval. If that division of labor interests you, it's literally what the products teach. Questions? Get in touch.
