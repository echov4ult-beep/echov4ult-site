You don't need more prompts. You need a team structure.

You're building a game alone, with an AI assistant that will happily do whatever you ask — which is exactly the problem. Nobody pushes back on the feature that breaks your pillars. Nobody re-reviews the crafting system after you "fixed" it at 1 a.m. Nobody remembers the decision you locked three weeks ago, so you re-litigate it every session.

Full Party installs the missing structure: defined roles, defined authority, and a paper trail. Ten specialist agents — Studio Director, Game Director, Art Director, Lead Engineer, Code Reviewer, QA Director, Quality Critic, Player Advocate, Narrative Designer, Marketing Strategist — each answering one question, each with defined boundaries, wired together by a governance system that says who decides what, how decisions get recorded, and what happens when they disagree.

It is not a game engine, not a framework, and not a prompt pack. It's a decision-making and quality-assurance layer that sits on top of whatever engine and tools you already use — Godot, Unity, Unreal, custom, or no engine at all.

The honest part

  • It doesn't write your game for you. It structures how your AI assistant works on your game. Provided as-is — no guarantee of functional code or commercial outcomes.
  • The Universal edition is real but less capable than the Claude Code edition: no file-system access, no slash commands. Same roles and governance, adapted for paste-in use.
  • If your project is a weekend jam and formal process would slow you down, skip the workflows for trivial changes. This kit exists to prevent expensive mistakes on consequential work.

Who this is NOT for

If you want an AI to generate a finished game from a one-line prompt, or you've never shipped past the prototype stage and don't want any process at all yet — this will feel like overhead, not help. Start with the free Demo Pack and see.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Does this work with my engine?

Any engine — Godot, Unity, Unreal, custom, or none (board games and design-only projects count). Onboarding asks about your engine and language so agents tailor their advice.

Do I need Claude Code?

No, but it's the best experience. The Universal Prompt edition works with any AI assistant that accepts text prompts.

Do I have to use all 10 agents?

No. Six cover a full concept-to-ship loop; "Single Specialist" mode lets you use one agent for one task without the governance pipeline.

Is this just prompts?

It's agent role definitions plus a governance system: authority map, decision log protocol, handoff rules, escalation playbooks, and a Definition of Done. The prompts are the smallest part.

What support do I get?

Installation assistance and product-defect support. Personalized game-dev consulting is not included. Full breakdown ships in the kit's SUPPORT_POLICY.md.

What about updates?

Free v1.x bug fixes, documentation improvements, and compatibility updates. Major v2+ releases may be paid upgrades, possibly with an existing-customer discount.

Can I customize it?

Yes — rename agents, add your own, remove ones you don't need, or rewrite the governance rules. They're markdown files you own; changes take effect immediately.

How do I try before buying?

The free Demo Pack: one agent, one workflow, one command, one template, one worked example, plus a manifest of exactly what Complete adds.
